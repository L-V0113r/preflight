## What the user experiences (PreFlight)

genuine page is **interrupted** (warned or asked). Unsure pages are not opened
silently: the user sees why and decides. Balanced is the default; Strict also asks
about every page it couldn't read or doesn't recognise.

## Honest uncertainty (the trade-off)

PreFlight answers **Unsure** instead of **Safe** when the model is undecided, or (in Strict,
or in Balanced when something else looks off) when it could not read the page or the page is
unlike its training data. Unsure pages are shown with an explanation rather than silently
loaded. Figures below are for Balanced:

- Benign pages answered Unsure: 2.3% overall, 0.1% of those whose content could be read
- Malicious pages answered Unsure (not flagged, but not called safe either): 9.8%
- Pages whose content could not be read, answered Unsure: 38.6%
